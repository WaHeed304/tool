About Toolstap

We make premium SEO software affordable so freelancers, agencies, and small teams can compete without enterprise budgets.

Why we built Toolstap

Retail seats for Ahrefs, SEMrush, Moz, and similar platforms add up fast. Toolstap exists so marketers can share access costs and still get industry-leading data for keyword research, backlinks, competitor analysis, and content production.

What we stand for

Honest pricing, clear tool lists per plan, secure shared access, and support that answers when something breaks. We focus on uptime and account health across the catalog so your research day stays productive.

Who we serve

Freelancers, bloggers, ecommerce sellers, and boutique agencies who need Ahrefs-level insight, Canva Pro creatives, Grammarly polish, and spy tools — without paying full retail for every seat.

Ready to join?

Pick a plan on our pricing page, complete signup, and unlock access — usually within seconds. Questions? Visit FAQ or contact us.

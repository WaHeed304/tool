This Privacy Policy (“Policy”) explains how Toolstap, the operator of seotoolsgroupbuy.us and related members access, handles your personal information. For the purposes of data protection law, Toolstap is the controller of your personal data.

We take your privacy seriously and take measures to protect your personal information. This Policy helps you understand how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you visit our website, create a members account, or use our SEO tools group buy Services. By visiting or using our website and Services, you agree to the terms below.

1. Collection and Use of Personal Information

Any personal information we receive will only be used to fulfill your order, provide group-buy access, or support our Services. We will not sell or redistribute your information to anyone.

  1. Purpose Identification: Before or at the time of collecting personal information, we identify the purposes for which it is collected (for example account signup, billing, or seat support).
  2. Use Limitations: We collect and use personal information solely for those purposes or compatible purposes, unless we obtain your consent or are required by law to do otherwise.
  3. Retention: We retain personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the identified purposes, including account history and support records.
  4. Lawful and Fair Collection: We collect personal information by lawful and fair means and, where appropriate, with your knowledge or consent.
  5. Data Relevance: Personal data should be relevant to the purposes for which it is used and, to the extent necessary, accurate, complete, and up to date.
  6. Security Safeguards: We protect personal information with reasonable security safeguards against loss, theft, and unauthorized access, disclosure, copying, use, or modification.
  7. Transparency: We make information about our policies and practices relating to personal information readily available to customers.

Information we may collect includes your name, email address, WhatsApp or contact number, billing details needed to process payments, plan selections, and messages you send to support about tool access or seats.

2. Payment Information

  1. Secure Payment Gateways: Payments are processed by secure third-party payment gateways (such as PayPal or card processors). Sensitive financial information is not entered directly into unprotected forms on our marketing site; transactions use SSL encryption.
  2. Limited Payment Details: We may collect and store limited payment information, such as payment method type, expiration date, and the last four digits of a card where provided by the processor. We do not collect or store full payment card numbers; full card data is handled by our third-party payment processors.

3. Use of Information

We use your personal information to:

  • Provide, analyze, administer, enhance, and personalize our Services and member portal access.
  • Process your registration, plan orders, renewals, upgrades, and payments.
  • Deliver tool seats, reset shared access when needed, and communicate about your account.
  • Prevent, detect, and investigate potentially prohibited or illegal activities, including fraud, and to enforce our Terms.
  • Analyze and understand our audience and improve our Service (including the website and members experience).

4. Google Analytics

Our website may use Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google, Inc. (“Google”).

  • Cookies: Google Analytics uses cookies to help the website analyze how users interact with the site. Information generated by the cookie about your use of the site (including your IP address) may be transmitted to and stored by Google on servers in the United States.
  • IP Anonymization: If IP anonymization is activated, Google truncates or anonymizes the last octet of the IP address for Member States of the European Union and other parties to the Agreement on the European Economic Area. In exceptional cases, the full IP address may be sent to and shortened by Google on servers in the U.S.
  • Usage: Google uses this information on our behalf to evaluate site use, compile reports on website activity, and provide related services. Google will not associate your IP address with other data held by Google except as described in Google’s policies.
  • Cookie Refusal: You may refuse cookies by selecting the appropriate settings in your browser. This may limit some website functionality.
  • Opt-Out: You can prevent Google’s collection and use of data by installing the browser plug-in at https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout?hl=en-GB.

For more details, please visit:

A cookie is an element of data that a website can send to your browser, which may then be stored on your system. Cookies used for basic site function do not store full payment card numbers. Our use of cookies helps identify sessions as you navigate our site, providing faster and more personalized access to Toolstap pages and the members experience where applicable.

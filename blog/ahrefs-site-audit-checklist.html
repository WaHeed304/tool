Overview

Toolstap members use shared and private seats so they can run this workflow without stacking every retail SaaS invoice. Fair-use keeps seats healthy; support restores access when vendors rotate credentials.

Why it matters

A practical crawl checklist freelancers run before handing reports to clients.

  • Real premium tools — legally purchased seats through the members portal
  • Fair monthly pricing — keep more budget for campaigns, not invoices
  • Fast support — WhatsApp and email when a seat needs help

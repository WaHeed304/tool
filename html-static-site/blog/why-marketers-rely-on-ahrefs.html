Why Marketers Still Rely on Ahrefs

Backlink maps, keyword gaps, and content ideas — here’s why Ahrefs remains a daily driver for serious SEO teams.

Alex RiveraUpdated Oct 18, 2026 · 5 min read · SEO Tips

Ahrefs remains the default for backlink intelligence and content gap research. Site Explorer and Keywords Explorer give marketers a clear picture of who links where — and which topics still have room to rank.

Why Ahrefs still wins daily workflows

For freelancers, the challenge is rarely whether Ahrefs is useful. It is whether the retail price fits every month. That is why so many Toolstap members keep Ahrefs in their stack through group buy access.

Features marketers use most

  • Backlink audits — map referring domains and lost links
  • Keyword research — volume, difficulty, and parent topics
  • Content ideas — see what already ranks for a competitor
  • Site audits — catch technical issues before they snowball

Ahrefs inside a Toolstap plan

Shared Ahrefs seats work well for research sessions and reporting. Follow fair-use, avoid heavy automation, and message support if a login needs a restore. Heavier teams can ask about a private seat.

Closing thoughts

Ahrefs earned its place by making link and keyword data actionable. With Toolstap, you can keep that workflow without absorbing the full retail invoice alone. Explore Ahrefs access or compare plans.

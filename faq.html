Frequently asked questions

Answers about plans, shared access, safety, payments, and support.

What is Toolstap / SEO Tools Group Buy?

Toolstap gives you affordable access to 90+ premium SEO, content writing, designer, Amazon, streaming, and spy tools. Group buy plans use shared accounts at a low monthly price. Private accounts are available separately.

How does group buy access work?

We purchase legitimate subscriptions and provide access through our members platform so you can use tools at a much lower cost than buying each one alone.

Is it safe to use?

Yes. We use legally purchased tool subscriptions and deliver access through a secure platform. There are no shady downloads. Shared seats are used on group buy plans; private accounts are a separate option.

Do you offer shared and private accounts?

Yes. Group buy plans use shared accounts for affordability. If you want a private account, contact support for pricing.

What tools are available?

90+ tools across SEO (AHREEF$, SEMRU$H, Moz, Majestic…), content & AI writing, design, Amazon, spy, and learning. Exact tools depend on your plan — Mega has the widest access.

How much does it cost?

Plans start at $10/month (Lite & Designer). Small and Writer are $15, Ahref$ Combo is $25, and Mega is $50. See the pricing page for full tool lists.

Do I get instant access after payment?

Yes. Once payment is confirmed you can start using the tools in your selected plan right away.

What kind of support do you provide?

Premium support through WhatsApp, live chat, email, and tickets. Reach us anytime from the contact page.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Plans are flexible with no long-term lock-in. Cancel before the next billing cycle if you no longer need access.
